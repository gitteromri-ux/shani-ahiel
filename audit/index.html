Top 10 mistakes

  1. Track record (Yafo row)“תחילה במיטה (Mitte)”: in Hebrew “במיטה” reads as “in bed”. It should be “במיטֶה (Mitte)” or “ברובע מיטה”.
  2. Stats + Track record“4 מותגי אירוח – שתי מסעדות ושני מלונות” / “ארבעה מותגים”: Selina and Amano are hotel chains she worked with, not her brands. This overclaims, and Atelier-level clients would spot it.
  3. Stats + Yafo row“200 מקומות ישיבה” is unverified and conflicts with the press. nomyblog says ~200 guests per day at old Yafo; Mux Mäuschen Wild gives the Ritterstraße space (today’s Yafo) as 45 seats inside + ~60 in the courtyard. Shani needs to confirm.
  4. Track record“Yafo 2015 – היום”: femtastics, The Berliner and nomyblog all say Yafo opened in 2016. Amano “2019–21” is also broader than the press, which describes a temporary Yafo pop-up at Amano around 2020–21.
  5. Hero eyebrow“מיפו לברלין” implies she is from Jaffa. The press says she’s from Tel Aviv; Yafo is the restaurant’s name. It’s misleading, so it should read “מתל אביב לברלין”.
  6. Stage section“קול מוביל בגסטרונומיה של ברלין”: an unverifiable hype claim. Also “עיר אחת שלא מוותרת לאף אחד” (Track record) is a meaningless cliché.
  7. Services / ContactVoice is inconsistent: “ספרו לי… ואחזור” vs “חוזרים אליכם תוך 24 שעות” vs “בואו נדבר”, and in EN “run by us” for a solo consultant. Mixed-language list items: “Hosting וחוויית אירוח”, “P&L”. “תוכנית פעולה ל-90 הימים הראשונים” was invented by us, not in her file.
  8. All eyebrows / nav brandRTL bug: Hebrew labels are set with wide letter-spacing (“מ י פ ו ל ב ר ל י ן”), which breaks Hebrew letterforms and reads as cheap. The Latin tagline “HOSPITALITY · F&B · BERLIN” under a Hebrew name is tiny gold on dark, so the contrast fails.
  9. Stage & print (clippings wall)13 low-res phone screenshots with iOS status bars, Facebook UI, yellow app headers and a “© Daliah Hoffmann” credit. It looks like a scrapbook, not a press page, and has licence risk. The hero uses a soft 1600px WhatsApp photo, darkened to mud, where most guests are looking at their phones. The Yafo row shows a random man in pink as “Yafo”.
  10. Mobile hero / everywhereThe WhatsApp floater covers content: hero-band labels (“לובי שהפך למסעדה”), form fields and article text. In the services lists some items render bolder than others (inconsistent weight). Press quotes are English on a Hebrew page, with the Hebrew translation demoted to grey 14px.

Top 5 design problems vs Atelier

  1. Restraint vs noise. Atelier uses one palette (forest green + cream/sage + a gold hairline) and one rhythm: light sections with a single dark hero/CTA band. Ours alternates ink / cream / paper / wine 9 times across 15 blocks, with 7 colours on screen (ink, cream, paper, gold, wine, rose, WhatsApp green).
  2. Effects & gimmicks. Atelier has essentially no motion. Ours stacks a Ken Burns hero, a gold frame box, a name ticker, a photo ticker, number counters, a progress bar, parallax, hover tilts and a pulsing WhatsApp ring. Each one reads as “template” and together they cheapen it.
  3. Type system. Atelier: one serif for display with an italic accent phrase in brand colour, a tight scale, centred headings, and small caps used only for Latin labels. Ours: thin Frank Ruhl 300 at 8rem, mixed with Heebo; letter-spaced Hebrew; four or more sizes of tiny grey/gold labels; heavy-weight inconsistencies.
  4. Photo treatment. Atelier: few photos, a clean founder portrait, logo-led hero, lots of space. Ours: 40 photos, many low-res or phone screenshots, aggressive dark overlays, repeated images (bar, candle and table appear twice), and masonry clutter.
  5. Structure / section count. Atelier: Hero → proof strip → founder → who we serve → services → work → method → CTA, with each section saying one thing. Ours: 15 blocks with overlapping jobs (stats + hero band + track record + stage + press all try to be “proof”; marquee + gallery + events mosaic all try to be “atmosphere”).

v3 plan

  • 8 sections only: Hero (type-led, her name + one line) → Proof strip (verified press logos) → About Shani (one great portrait, 3 short paragraphs) → Services (4 tracks, one clean grid) → Track record (4 rows, years + one line each, no stats overclaim) → Method (4 steps, one line each) → Journal (3 cards) → Contact (form + WhatsApp). Drop the marquee, photo ticker, events mosaic, clippings wall and numbers counter.
  • Palette: warm off-white (#F6F1EA) + deep ink (#16120F) + a single accent, Shishi-rosé/wine (#7A2333) used only for italics and buttons, with a gold hairline at most. Light page; one dark band, for the contact CTA only.
  • Type: Frank Ruhl Libre 400/500 for headings (no 300), Assistant or Heebo 400 for body at 18px/1.75 in near-black. No letter-spacing on Hebrew. One italic-style accent phrase per heading in the accent colour (Atelier’s signature move).
  • Hero: calm and typographic: “שני אחיאל” + “ייעוץ, הקמה וניהול F&B למסעדות ולמלונות” + one CTA. Beside it, one high-quality portrait (the white-shirt/flowers shot, which is her best and on-brand). No dark overlay, frame or Ken Burns.
  • Photos: 6–8 max, all hi-res. Request originals from Shani (the WhatsApp copies are compressed) plus the fizzz cover as a file. Show press as clean outlet wordmarks with links (The Infatuation, The Berliner, femtastics, Mit Vergnügen, fizzz, stern, Berliner Zeitung), not screenshots.
  • Copy rewrite (Hebrew first, native-level): one voice (first person, “אני”), fix “במיטה”, remove the overclaims (“4 brands”, “leading voice”, invented 90-day plan), correct the years and seat count after Shani confirms, and no mixed-language list items. EN mirrors it with “I”, not “we”.
  • Motion: a single subtle fade-up on scroll, nothing else. The WhatsApp button becomes a small, quiet circle that hides over the form and footer on mobile.
  • Gate before shipping: screenshots at 1440/390 compared side by side with Atelier, and a native Hebrew proofread. Show Omri a static preview link first, then publish.
