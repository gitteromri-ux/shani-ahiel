About

Everything I advise on, I have built myself.

I started behind the bar and on the floor in Tel Aviv, moved into event management, and in Berlin built Yafo from the ground up: a 200-seat restaurant and bar that became one of the city’s best-known addresses for Israeli hospitality. Then came Shishi, a chef-led farm-to-table restaurant, and partnerships with hotels: turning the lobby at Amano into a true restaurant, and leading the entire food & beverage operation at Selina Berlin.

Today I bring that experience to restaurant owners, founders and hotels: operating structure, cost control, teams that know how to host, and guest experiences people come back for. No theory – only what has been proven in front of real guests, every night.
