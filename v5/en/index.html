About

From Tel Aviv to Berlin.

I started out on the floor and behind the bar in Tel Aviv, then moved into event management. In Berlin I founded Yafo, a restaurant and bar with weekly events, followed by Shishi, a chef-led restaurant that worked directly with farmers.

I went on to work with hotels: at Amano we turned the lobby into a true restaurant, and at Selina Berlin I led the entire food & beverage operation against measurable targets, in a partnership model.

Everything I offer clients, I have done myself first, as an owner and operator. Today I work with restaurant owners, founders and hotels who want a business that works, and makes money.

Yafo · Berlin

The restaurant Berlin queued for.

In Berlin I founded Yafo, a restaurant and bar with weekly events that became a well-known address for Israeli hospitality in the city. Long tables, candlelight, a red ceiling, and a bar people end up dancing on.

“Even on weeknights, people wait at the door for a table.”Süddeutsche Zeitung, 2017, on Yafo
Shishi · Berlin

The chef’s restaurant I founded after Yafo.

After Yafo I founded Shishi, a farm-to-table chef’s restaurant that worked directly with Brandenburg farmers. Long candlelit tables, velvet curtains, and a brick courtyard that fills up on summer nights.
